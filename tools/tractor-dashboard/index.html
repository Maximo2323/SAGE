00 / ROUTE · SIM


Drive: wheel distance, − = reverse. Turn: + = left; rolls forward until the IMU heading is within 2°. Find: drives until the sensor reading comes into (seen) or goes out of (lost) mm ± tol, then backs over that edge and comes up to it again at 10 %; fails after max m. The reading is corrected for the car angle (× cos of heading − route heading). hold > 0 (side sensors): while it sees the object, P control on the heading (kp ° per mm, at most 20°) keeps that distance. after > 0: once the edge is read, drive on that many m (still holding), then stop; max then limits only the search for the edge. Align: after a Find, drives from that edge to the checkpoint (From map fills in the distance; it assumes the car arrives straight along the checkpoint heading), stops and logs the error. Checkpoint: stops, logs the error to the checkpoint with that label and waits. Obstacles (Drive, side-sensor Find): front < 40 cm = stop, wait 5 s; still there = swerve left past its edge + half the car + 20 cm, pass it, swerve back to the line. A new obstacle during the pass or the swerve back: stop, wait; still there = back up and go around both. Values: AVOID_* in constants.h. Each checkpoint on the map shows what the sensors read there (F / RL / RH). Live: the car runs the route flashed in its firmware. Save the route here, then build and flash. Auto starts it; Manual, Space or a drive key stops it.
